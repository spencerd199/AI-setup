# Traffic and Sales Plan

**Why sales are slow, and what to do about it — in order.**

## The diagnosis
```
Views -> Visits -> Orders
```
- **Views are the failing stage.** Marketplace search shows the listings to almost no one.
- **Conversion is fine.** Buyers who reach a listing buy at a healthy rate. The listings, photos and prices aren't the problem.

**So the funnel is healthy everywhere except the top, where it's nearly empty.**

## What's NOT the problem (checked, not assumed)
- **Listing quality** — the marketplace's own listing-quality tool rates all 18 listings as complete.
- **Price** — mid-market per [[Pricing Model]].
- **Ad budget** — paid ads spend a small fraction of their daily cap because there aren't enough matching searches to spend it on. More budget changes nothing.

## The plan, in order
- [ ] **1. More listings from existing designs.** New aspect ratios, color variants and set combinations. The search surface grows with listing count, and this needs no new artwork.
- [ ] **2. A visual-discovery platform** — pin every listing, then claim the shop domain so pins earn attribution.
- [ ] **3. Then** revisit paid ads, once there's enough inventory for the budget to spend.
- [ ] **4. Only then** a new drop.

## Raised and declined — don't re-raise
- Short-form video as a channel — declined.
- A welcome discount — declined; reasoning in the pricing note.
