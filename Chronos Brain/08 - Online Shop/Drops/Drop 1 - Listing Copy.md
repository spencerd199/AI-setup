## Title formula
`[What it is] - [the specific subjects] - [style], [room], Digital Download`

Front-load the words a buyer actually types. The shop name never goes in a title — nobody searches for a shop they haven't heard of.

**Example:**
```
Botanical Wall Art Set of 6 - Fern, Eucalyptus & Wildflower Prints - Watercolor Decor, Living Room, Digital Download
```

## Tags
13 per listing, each 20 characters or fewer.

> **Method banked 2026-09-14: enter tags one at a time.** Pasting all 13 at once silently dropped the last few — the page accepted the input and showed no error. **Type one tag, press Return, wait for it to appear, then the next.** Re-read the saved listing to confirm all 13 before moving on.
