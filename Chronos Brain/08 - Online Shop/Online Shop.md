# Online Shop

**Paper Lantern Studio** — printable wall art sold as instant digital downloads on an online marketplace. *(Fictional business.)* No inventory: design once, sell repeatedly.

## Folders in this folder
- **`Research/`** → [[Research]] — market and trend research that picks each drop's direction.
- **`Drops/`** → [[Drops]] — per-drop design direction and paste-ready listing copy.
- **`Shop/`** → [[Shop]] — pricing, security rules, and the traffic plan.

## Where it stands
- **Live since 2026-08-10.** 18 listings across three drops.
- **5 sales, $52 total.** Buyers who reach a listing convert; the problem is getting found.
- The daily shop-manager agent checks the shop at noon and reports. It never edits a listing. See [[Agents & Jobs]].

## The one constraint
**Discovery.** Marketplace search sends almost no traffic yet. The plan to fix it is [[Traffic and Sales Plan]], not another drop.
