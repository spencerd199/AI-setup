# Listing Setup Plan

**Goal:** list the property for the off-season and get to a first booking without taking on risk nobody has priced.

## Answer these before anything goes live
- [ ] **Ownership and pay structure with [[Sam]].** Options: a flat management fee, a profit split, or equity in an LLC. **Undefined defaults to a job, not an asset** — decide first.
- [ ] **Mortgage and HOA terms** — confirm short-term rentals are allowed.
- [ ] **Insurance** — a standard homeowner's policy usually excludes rental use. Get a quote for a short-term rental policy.
- [ ] **Local permits** — confirm the town's rules in writing, not by phone.
- [ ] **Turnover cleaning** — who does it, and at what cost per stay.

## Then, in order
1. Professional photos (schedule after the questions above are settled).
2. Listing copy — drafted by Chronos, edited by me.
3. Pricing: start 10% under comparable listings for the first five reviews.
4. Go live.
