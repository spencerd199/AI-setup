# Trading Rules

**These rules are locked.** Changing one is a deliberate decision made on a quiet day, never mid-trade.

## Position sizing
- No single position above 15% of the portfolio.
- Risk no more than 1% of the portfolio on any one trade's stop.

## Entry
- Write the thesis in [[Trade Journal]] **before** placing the order. No thesis, no trade.
- No trades in the first 15 minutes after the open.
- No entries within three days of a company's earnings report.

## What I don't trade
- Anything I can't explain in two sentences.
- Leveraged ETFs held overnight.
