# Investing

My personal portfolio. **The rules come before the trades** — every entry in the journal is checked against them.

- [[Trading Rules]] — position sizing, entry criteria, and what I don't trade. Exit rules still missing.
- [[Trade Journal]] — one row per trade: the thesis at entry, the result, and what I'd do differently.

**The AI's role here is a second pair of eyes, not a signal service.** It checks a proposed trade against the written rules and flags where it breaks one. It never recommends a position.
