# Session 3 — 2026-09-15

**Format:** CONVERGENT — take the strongest live idea and try to kill it.

## Territory
Digital products for people who cook at home.

## A friction from this week
Dinner planning kept getting pushed to the last minute this week. The workday starts after a long morning walk with [[Max]], and by evening nobody has decided what to cook.

## What actually changed
Nothing new in the tools this week that bears on this territory. **Saying so is the honest answer** — this section surfaces real changes, not invented ones.

## Pre-check — who already sells this
Plenty of meal-planning templates exist. **Almost all are built for families** — four-serving recipes, bulk shopping lists. Very few are built for one person.

---

## Answers
**On the target customer:** "Cooking for one is the whole pitch. Everyone else is selling to families."

**On the format:** "Printable and spreadsheet versions, same listing. Don't make people choose."

## Outcome
**Survives, narrowed.** Target customer is now people cooking for one. Re-scored 6.5 → 7.0 in [[Idea Ledger]].

**What would kill it:** if the first five listings get no views after two weeks, even with the shop's existing traffic.
