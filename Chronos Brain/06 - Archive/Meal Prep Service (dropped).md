# Meal Prep Service (dropped)

A weekly meal-prep delivery service for young professionals nearby.

## Why it was dropped
- **It fails the core test in [[Idea Ledger]]:** it owns no asset that earns without me. Every unit of revenue needed my time in a kitchen.
- Food-handling permits and a commercial kitchen rental pushed break-even past 60 customers a week.

## What would bring it back
- A partner who wants to run the kitchen, turning it from a labor business into an ownership stake. Until then, it stays here.
