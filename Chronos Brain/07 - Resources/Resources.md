# Resources

Cross-project reference material — the things that serve every project rather than one.

## Folders in this folder
- **`Agents & Jobs/`** → [[Agents & Jobs]] — the scheduled agents Chronos runs, one folder per job, each with its instructions, its runner script, and its heartbeat log. **Start there for anything about how the automation works.**

## Notes in this folder
- [[Pending Approvals]] — everything an agent has drafted and is deliberately refusing to send. One queue across every job.
- [[Skill Self-Improvement Section]] — **the standard `## Self-Improvement` block every skill must carry, plus the rules that keep it safe.** After a bug or blocker is solved and the fix confirmed, the skill records symptom, cause, fix and dead end in its own `Known Issues & Fixes`, and reads them before its next run.
