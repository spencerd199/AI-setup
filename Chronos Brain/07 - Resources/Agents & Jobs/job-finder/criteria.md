# Job Finder — screening criteria

Each role is scored out of 10. **7.0 is the pass mark.** A pass means worth Spencer's attention, not worth applying to.

| Criterion | Weight | Fails outright if |
|---|---|---|
| Role fit | 3 | Not an analyst or associate-level finance role |
| Location | 2 | Based outside Chicago or another large city *(fail rule added 2026-09-09)* |
| Experience required | 2 | Asks for 3+ years of full-time experience |
| Firm quality | 2 | No verifiable track record, or pay is commission-only |
| Deadline realism | 1 | Closes within 3 days of the run that found it |

## Amendments
- **2026-09-09:** relocation now fails the Location row outright. A role requiring a move out of the target cities scored 7.1 and passed, because the old criteria had no rule for it. Spencer declined it in [[Pending Approvals]].
